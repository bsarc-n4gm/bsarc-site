## Support BSARC: Join or Renew Your ARRL Membership!

Did you know you can join or renew your ARRL membership directly through the club? When you do, **BSARC receives a commission** ($15.00 for first-time signups or $5.00 for renewals) to help fund club activities!

To register through the club:
1. Download and complete the [ARRL Club Commission Membership Application](https://www.arrl.org/files/file/Membership/Club%20Commission/ARRL%20Membership%20Application%20CLUB%20rev%20Jan%202024.pdf).
2. Mail or deliver the completed form alongside a check made out to **ARRL** directly to our Club Treasurer:
