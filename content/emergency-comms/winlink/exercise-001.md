### 🛠️ Assigned Task Flow
Draft a basic outbound text payload string, attach your current operational station settings, and transmit the packet data via **Telnet or RF** paths straight to **`K1MJJ`**.

1. Open your **Winlink Express** client interface.
2. Select **Message &rarr; New Message** from the standard toolbar header options.
3. In the **To:** recipient data container box, input: `K1MJJ`
4. Set the subject line header parameters exactly as: `Brunswick County Winlink Net Session 001`
5. In the clear plain-text body message canvas area, provide the following mandatory metrics records string lines:
   * **Line 1:** `This is an official training exercise payload message.`
   * **Line 2:** Your station callsign, first name, current city location, and county area metrics.
   * **Line 3:** Your home QTH station exact Latitude and Longitude variables to map grid convergence tracking vectors.
6. Click **Post to Outbox** on the editor command menu layout.
7. Initialize an operational link session panel block via either **Telnet Winlink** or a direct over-the-air RF pipeline, then click **Start** to clear the message queue tracking registers.
