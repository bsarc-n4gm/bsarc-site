# New to Amateur Radio? Welcome!

Amateur Radio (or "Ham Radio") isn't just about talking on local repeaters. Today, it is a high-tech, cutting-edge hobby that sits at the intersection of computer science, emergency resilience, and outdoor adventure. 

Whether you are a tech tinkerer, a cybersecurity enthusiast, or an outdoor adventurer, there is a place for you here.

---

### 🚀 What Can You Do with a Modern License?

* **Explore Software Defined Radio (SDR) & RF Hacking:** Connect a simple $25 USB dongle to your PC and legally decode live aircraft data, track weather satellites, or intercept digital data streams right from your desk.
* **Go Off-Grid with Parks on the Air (POTA):** Combine hiking, camping, and overlanding with portable, battery-powered radio gear. Reach the other side of the world from a mountain trail or a local state park picnic table.
* **Talk to Space:** Did you know you can bounce signals off the moon, track amateur satellites, or even talk directly to astronauts aboard the International Space Station using gear you can build yourself?
* **Digital Modes & Decentralized Networks:** Run high-speed microwave mesh networks (AREDN) or use low-power digital modes like FT8 to make global contacts using just your computer and a radio.

---

### 🛡️ Emergency Resilience & Community Service
When hurricanes strike the Carolinas, power grids fail, and cell towers go dark, ham radio operators are the ones providing the ultimate backup communications infrastructure. Through **ARES (Amateur Radio Emergency Service)**, our club helps keep local emergency management and EMS connected when everything else fails.
